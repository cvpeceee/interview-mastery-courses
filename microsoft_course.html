Microsoft Software Engineer Interview Mastery

L63+ / Senior level. Clean, testable code; growth mindset; team-driven loops. Textbook-style guide for interviews and strong performance.

Recommended focused time: 45–65 hours

1. Interview Process & Levels

Time: 5–7 hours

Typical stages

  • Recruiter screen
  • Online assessment or technical phone screen
  • Loop of 4–5 interviews (coding, design, behavioral)
  • Possible “As Appropriate” (AA) round with a senior leader

Loops are team-driven. Expect variation. System design becomes more important at senior levels (L62+).

Level guidance

  • L59–L61: Heavier coding focus
  • L62–L63: Coding + design
  • L64+: Deeper design, leadership, and ambiguity handling

2. Coding Interview Deep Dive

Time: 16–22 hours

What Microsoft evaluates

  • Problem solving
  • Clean, readable, maintainable code
  • Testing and edge-case coverage (explicitly valued)
  • Communication of approach

Problems are typically medium difficulty. Some teams include object-oriented or class design.

Practice emphasis
Narrate your approach before coding. After writing the solution, explicitly test it with examples and edge cases. Interviewers notice when you proactively test.

3. Design (System or Low-Level)

Time: 12–16 hours

Clarify the style early

  • System design: scalable services (notifications, storage, chat, rate limiter)
  • Low-level design: class design for systems like parking lot, elevator, or file system

Ask in the first minute which style the interviewer wants. Senior roles lean more toward system design.

4. Behavioral & Growth Mindset

Time: 6–10 hours

Key themes

  • Growth mindset — learning from mistakes and feedback
  • Collaboration across teams (One Microsoft)
  • Customer focus
  • Deep dives on past projects and technical decisions
Prepare at least one strong story about learning from a failure or mistake. Microsoft interviewers value this highly.

5. On the Job Excellence

Time: Ongoing

What strong engineers do

  • Write clear, testable, maintainable code
  • Seek feedback and improve iteratively
  • Collaborate effectively across organizational boundaries
  • Document decisions and trade-offs
  • Focus on customer impact

6. Full Preparation Plan

Time: Ongoing
  1. Coding with emphasis on quality and testing (16–22 hrs)
  2. Design practice (both system and low-level)
  3. Behavioral stories, especially growth mindset
  4. Full mock loops
