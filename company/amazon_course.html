Amazon Software Engineer Interview Mastery

SDE II / L6+ level. Leadership Principles are weighted as heavily as technical skills. Bar Raiser has veto power.

Recommended focused time: 50–70 hours

1. Interview Process & Bar Raiser

Time: 5–7 hours

Typical stages

  • Online Assessment (coding + work simulation + work style survey) for many roles
  • Phone screen (coding + LP questions)
  • Loop of 4–5 interviews (55–60 min each)
  • Bar Raiser — cross-team interviewer with veto power whose job is to maintain the hiring bar
Almost every round includes Leadership Principle questions. Strong coding with weak LP stories often leads to rejection.

2. Leadership Principles (Highest ROI)

Time: 12–16 hours

Prepare thoroughly

Amazon evaluates against all 16 Leadership Principles. For engineering roles the most frequently tested include:

  • Customer Obsession
  • Ownership
  • Dive Deep
  • Bias for Action
  • Invent and Simplify
  • Deliver Results
  • Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
  • Earn Trust

Prepare 15–20 detailed STAR stories that can map to multiple principles. Include metrics and clear outcomes. Expect deep follow-up questions.

Example probes
• Tell me about a time you took ownership of something outside your job description.
• Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision and what you did.
• Tell me about a time you went deep to find a root cause.

3. Coding Preparation

Time: 15–20 hours

Focus

  • Medium LeetCode-style problems (arrays, graphs, trees, DP, strings)
  • Object-oriented design appears more often than at some peers
  • Coding rounds are usually paired with LP questions, so manage time

4. System Design

Time: 10–15 hours

For SDE II / L5 and above

  • Practical distributed systems
  • AWS-informed thinking is a plus
  • Clarify requirements, discuss trade-offs and failure modes
Examples: Design a notification system, rate limiter, storage service, or feature at Amazon scale.

5. Preparation Plan

Time: Ongoing
  1. Leadership Principles (12–16 hrs) — Highest priority. Build and rehearse stories.
  2. Coding practice (medium difficulty)
  3. System design for L5+
  4. Full loop mocks including Bar Raiser style probing
Many candidates fail Amazon loops because of weak LP stories even when coding is solid. Prioritize this.
